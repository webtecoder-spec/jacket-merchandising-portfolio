Process

Merchandising Cycle

Complete ownership from buyer requirement through shipment execution

01

Buyer Requirement

Receive and analyze buyer brief, style requirements, target price and delivery timeline.

02

Technical Package Review

Review tech pack, construction details, measurement specs and quality standards.

03

BOM / CAD / IE Consumption

Coordinate bill of materials, CAD marker and industrial engineering consumption data.

04

Costing

Develop accurate garment costing covering fabric, accessories, SMV and overheads.

05

Fabric & Accessories Booking

Book shell, lining, padding, zippers, trims and all required materials with suppliers.

06

Sample Development

Manage proto, fit, size set and pre-production samples to meet buyer standards.

07

Buyer Comments / Approval

Collect, analyze and implement buyer comments until final sample approval.

08

Bulk Material Follow-up

Track material in-house status, quality and readiness for bulk production start.

09

Production

Monitor cutting, sewing, finishing against T&A and capacity plans.

10

Quality & Testing

Coordinate inline and final inspection plus required laboratory testing.

11

Shipment

Manage commercial documents, packing list, booking and on-time delivery.
